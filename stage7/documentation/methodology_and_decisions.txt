Q2 EXACT ACCOUNTING
For lag k=1 or12,C_g,t=100*w_g*(I_g,t-I_g,t-k)/H_t-k. Official simple headline inflation is100*(H_t/H_t-k-1). Add R_t=headline change minus sum of the six group contributions. Share_g=100*Cov(C_g,headline)/Var(headline). Shares including R sum exactly100. Cross-covariances are included. Shares can be negative or exceed100. This is a sample accounting allocation,not a causal percentage,variance of an identified shock,or a CPI basket weight.
Raw MoM and YoY official-headline allocations are primary. Legacy90.589851% is separately reproduced for a group-built SA aggregate.
